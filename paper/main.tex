\begin{abstract}
Deep learning models for 3D medical image segmentation rely on large-scale
annotated datasets that are costly to curate. Data owners may wish to protect
their proprietary medical images from unauthorized exploitation. We propose
\textbf{3DMedUE}, a method that generates unlearnable examples specifically
designed for volumetric medical images. By adding imperceptible perturbations
to 3D scans, models trained on the protected data fail to learn meaningful
representations, resulting in near-zero segmentation performance. Experiments
on BraTS19 (brain tumor) and FLARE21 (abdominal organ) benchmarks demonstrate
that 3DMedUE reduces victim model performance to nearly zero across all
metrics, significantly outperforming existing 2D unlearnable example methods
adapted to the 3D setting.
%
\keywords{Unlearnable examples \and Medical image segmentation \and Data protection}
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}

We evaluate on two public 3D medical segmentation benchmarks:
\textbf{BraTS19}~\cite{menze2014multimodal} for brain tumor segmentation
(3 classes: NCR/NET, ED, ET) and \textbf{FLARE21}~\cite{ma2022fast} for
abdominal organ segmentation (4 classes: liver, kidney, spleen, pancreas).
The victim model is a 3D~UNet. We compare against several unlearnable example
baselines: random Gaussian noise, Min-Min~\cite{huang2021unlearnable},
TAP~\cite{fowl2021adversarial}, LSP~\cite{yu2022availability}, and
SEP~\cite{liu2023stable}.

We report four metrics: Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC), Intersection over
Union (IoU), 95\% Hausdorff Distance (HD95), and Average Surface Distance
(ASD). For effective data protection, DSC and IoU should be \emph{lower}
(worse segmentation), while HD95 and ASD should be \emph{higher}.

\subsection{Main Results}\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:main} summarizes the protection effectiveness.
On BraTS19, 3DMedUE reduces DSC and IoU to nearly 0\%, while increasing
HD95 to 45.56\,mm and ASD to 31.02\,mm, vastly outperforming all baselines.
Existing methods such as Min-Min and SEP only achieve partial degradation
(DSC still above 69\%). On FLARE21, 3DMedUE similarly achieves strong
protection with DSC reduced to 6.8\%.
